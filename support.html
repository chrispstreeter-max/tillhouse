Support

What support covers

  • Questions about Tillhouse: setting up the theme, its sections, blocks and settings, the bundle builder and the trade portal.
  • Bug fixes: if something in Tillhouse doesn't work as documented (a broken layout, a dead link, a logic error), we fix it in a theme update. Critical bugs are fixed straight away.
  • Reply time: within two business days.

What support doesn't cover

  • Third-party apps and their code.
  • Changes to the theme's code made by you or someone else.
  • Custom design or development work, store setup outside the theme, or help moving content from another theme. We can quote for these separately as paid work.

Before editing theme code, always duplicate the theme (Online Store › Themes › … › Duplicate). For bigger customizations, consider hiring a Shopify Partner.

Most answers are in the documentation, the FAQ and Troubleshooting.
