Questions

Do I need any apps?

No. The bundle builder uses Shopify's own automatic discounts, and the trade portal uses Shopify B2B. Both are built into the theme.

What does the trade portal need?

Shopify B2B. On Basic, Grow and Advanced plans, trade prices are set per B2B market. Per-customer pricing needs Shopify Plus. The theme never stores or calculates a trade price; Shopify does.

Does bundle pricing work in every country?

Bundles work in markets that sell at your store prices. Where a market changes the price (a price adjustment, price list or another currency), the builder tells shoppers bundle pricing isn't available in their region.

Where do I buy it?

On the Shopify Theme Store. You pay through Shopify, and you can try Tillhouse in your store before you buy.

What support do I get?

Email support covering setup questions and theme bugs. It doesn't cover third-party apps or custom code changes. Updates are released through the Shopify Theme Store.

Are the demo photos included?

Yes, as demo content under the Unsplash License, so your store looks complete while you build. Replace them with your own photography before launch.
