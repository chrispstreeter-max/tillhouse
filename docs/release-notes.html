Changelog

6.0.1

  • New name: the theme is now Tillhouse by CHRISPSDesign. It was briefly called Counter, which is also the name of a Shopify app.
  • Bundle builder in other markets: where a market sells the bundle items at a different price (a price adjustment, price list or other currency), the builder now says bundle pricing isn't available in that region, instead of showing an empty list.
  • The trade order page shows the company name once when the location has the same name.

6.0.0 — Tillhouse (renamed from Master Custom Build)

  • New name: the theme is now Tillhouse by CHRISPSDesign. Your licence, settings and content carry over unchanged.
  • Bundle builder (optional module): shoppers mix and match single items and unlock tier prices ("any 3 for £27"). It includes a builder page, a "Build a bundle" product block, a collection button, a homepage promo section and bundle grouping in the cart. Prices are set by Shopify discounts; the theme never changes a price. See the Bundle Builder Guide.
  • Trade portal (optional module): a wholesale landing page with an application form, plus a trade order page for Shopify B2B buyers. The order page shows catalog prices, minimums, case sizes, maximums, volume pricing and repeat last order, with order-minimum guidance in the cart. Nothing is shown to non-trade visitors. See the Trade Portal Guide.
  • Module demo: the Demo Content pack adds 4 chocolate bars and MODULES-DEMO.md, a step-by-step setup that shows both modules working on the Food & Beverage demo.
  • Store header layout: a full-width search bar with live results, an "All products" category menu, shortcut links with optional HOT tags and an accordion menu on phones. Its styles load only when you use it.
  • 8 new sections: Brand statement, Brand bento, Shop lanes, Product families, Product rail, Editorial collage, Community quotes and Newsletter band.
  • Improvements:
    • Featured collection takes hand-picked product blocks.
    • Featured product spotlight options.
    • New promo-tile layouts (four across, natural height, swipe on phones).
    • A footer legal notice.
    • A "solid" quick-add bar option.
    • Payment-icon options.
    • Better contrast for form messages and small labels.
    • Predictive-search accessibility fixes.

5.0.0 — Master Custom Build V5

  • Motion intensity: Off, Subtle, Standard or Expressive scales every animation. Reduced-motion preferences are always respected.
  • Mobile bottom navigation (optional): Home, Shop, Search, Wishlist and Cart within thumb reach. It works with the sticky add-to-cart bar.
  • Recent searches in the search panel, stored only on the shopper's device and clearable.
  • Wishlist: share link, save a shared list and add every available item to the cart in one tap.
  • Account page: shows the wishlist and recently viewed products.
  • Collections: Load more or Infinite scroll. Page numbers stay in the page for search engines.
  • Pre-order: variants that keep selling at zero stock show Pre-order and add a visible note to the order.
  • New sections: FAQ (with optional FAQ structured data) and Comparison table. New Design-section presets: Stats and Timeline.
  • New theme styles: Conversion, High-impact, Product-focused and Mobile-first.
  • Demo switcher: categories, descriptions and a mobile bottom sheet.
  • Performance budget: enforced by the QA suite.

4.0.0 — Master Custom Build by CHRISPSDesign

  • Renamed from M.Leroy Master to Master Custom Build by CHRISPSDesign.
  • 8 storefront demos on one engine: Fashion, Beauty, Streetwear, Electronics, Home & Living, Luxury, Food & Beverage and General Retail.
    • Each demo has its own theme style (fonts, palette, buttons, cards, spacing, header and footer), alternate homepage, bundled photography and announcement bar.
  • Demo switcher: visitors can preview every demo across all pages. Only the active demo's styles and fonts load.
  • Demo Content pack: 80 products with variants, stock and photography, and 40 collections, imported with Shopify's product CSV.
  • New Demos visibility condition, so sections and announcement bars can target specific demos.
  • New Style directions: Beauty, Streetwear, Electronics & tech and Food & beverage.
  • Featured collection can show hand-picked products instead of a collection.
  • Accent badges choose readable text automatically. Multi-line text metafields keep their line breaks.

3.0.0 — Final release

Product pages

  • Two-column layout, gallery left by default: column widths 40/60 to 65/35 or custom, content width, column gap, tablet stacked or two columns, scrolling (auto, sticky information, sticky gallery, normal), alignment and information width.
  • Gallery: thumbnails left, right or below; single image, grid, stacked or masonry; ratio, fit, gaps, counter, arrows, hover zoom, lightbox; phone gallery edge to edge, inside margins or with thumbnails.
  • Every component is a draggable block: breadcrumbs, subtitle, rating with breakdown, price with compare-at and savings, stock, variant picker (buttons, pills, dropdown, swatches; separate Size and Colour selectors), Buy it now, payment messaging, pickup, tabs, Complete the look, recommended products and more.
  • Storytelling presets: Product story, Video with text, Full-width image and video, Features, Materials & care, Specifications, Testimonial, Before / after story; new Quote block.
  • Empty blocks render nothing; no editor notices on product pages.

Merchandising

  • Promotion block (live offer or message only) for any block area; collection grid Text, Product, Video and Offer tiles with widths and heights per device.
  • Badge rules 1–4 (tag, collection, type, vendor) with Trending and Staff pick ready to use; custom badge styles, size and icons.

Search

  • Opens as a panel, drawer or full screen; popular searches, quick links and featured products before typing; choose predictive result types and product count.

Design system and editor

  • Typography letter spacing works as designed; new Navigation typography level; small labels follow Caption.
  • Custom button size, card shadow, form field style/height/focus, default image crop position.
  • Show on desktop / tablet / mobile for every block; product-state visibility condition; sections and blocks grouped by purpose.
  • Before / after as a block as well as a section.

Fixes

  • Offer panels styled on every page; tier boxes readable in dark panels; cart upsell and search panels no longer show empty headings; invented sample copy, default searches and policy text removed.
