Complete Theme Guide

Tillhouse is a modular Shopify theme: every page is made of sections you add, remove and reorder, and most sections are made of blocks you can add, remove, reorder, duplicate and hide. Store-wide design — fonts, colours, buttons, spacing — is set once in Theme settings and followed everywhere.

How the theme is organised

Layer Where What it controls
Theme settings Theme Editor › brush icon The design system (style direction, colours, typography, buttons, cards, forms, spacing, media, animation), badges, promotions, search, cart, social links, SEO.
Header and footer Header / Footer groups in the sidebar Announcement bar, header, footer — shared by every page.
Templates Page selector at the top of the editor Home page, product templates, collection templates, cart, search, blog, pages, customer pages.
Sections Add section on each template Full-width bands: hero, product grid, image with text, before/after…
Blocks Add block inside a section Headings, text, images, buttons, product information components, tiles…

Add section and Add block list everything in groups: Layout, Commerce, Media, Content, Marketing, Social proof, Storytelling, Navigation, Product page and Advanced.

Home page

Build the home page from any combination of sections. The most useful:

Goal Section
Opening statement Slideshow, Hero (editorial, split, full-width image, video, promotion banner)
Promotions Promotional banner (full image, split, boxed, slim bar, countdown), Promotion tiles
Shop categories Collection list (grid or mosaic), Design section › Category Grid
Show products Product grid (grid, carousel, editorial), Featured product, Recently viewed
Tell your story Image with text, Design section presets: Story Section, Image with Text, Product story, Video with text, Full-width image / video, Features
Compare Before / after section, or the Before / after story preset
Social proof Testimonials (quotes and press logos), Design section › Testimonial, Brand Grid (logos), Guarantees
Capture emails Newsletter, Design section › Newsletter
Anything else Design section — a blank canvas of blocks with columns per device

No demo content is required: every section works with your own images and text, and empty image slots show neutral placeholder artwork only until you add images.

Header and navigation

  • Announcement bar — rotating messages, offer announcements, optional country/language selector.
  • Header — five layouts: Standard (logo left, menu centre), Inline (logo and menu left), Editorial (logo centre, menu below), Minimal (menu button only) and Campaign (with a call-to-action button). Options: menu and alignment, highlighted item (e.g. Sale), sticky (off, always, on scroll up), open menus on hover, full width, line below, search / account / country & language icons, cart icon, colour schemes and transparent over the home page.
  • Mega menus — add Mega menu blocks to the Header and name the top-level menu item: link columns with feature images, with products, with a promotion panel, or collection image tiles.
  • Mobile — phones and tablets use a menu button and a drill-down menu drawer with account, country/language and social links.

Search

Theme settings › Search: search opens as a panel from the top, a side drawer or full screen (phones always full screen). Before typing it can show your popular searches, a quick-links menu and featured products; while typing, predictive results for suggestions, products, collections and pages/articles — each switchable. The Search page has the same filters and sorting as collections.

Product pages

Two columns — gallery left, product information right by default — built from draggable blocks: breadcrumbs, title, subtitle, rating, price, badges, stock, variant pickers, quantity, buy buttons, Buy it now, payment messaging, delivery, pickup, offers, size guide, accordions, tabs, description, specifications, Complete the look, related products, siblings, share and more. See the Product Page Guide.

Collections

  • Collection banner — text, split or image overlay layouts, description, product count, quick-link pills, breadcrumbs.
  • Collection grid — products per page, columns for desktop, tablet and mobile, density switcher, filters in a sidebar or drawer (Shopify Search & Discovery), sorting, sticky toolbar, pagination.
  • Editorial tiles inside the grid — Image, Text, Product, Video and Offer tiles, each with its position, desktop width (1–3 columns), phone width and height (1–2 rows).
  • Collection templates can add any other section above or below the grid.

Product cards

One card everywhere (collections, search, grids, recommendations): hover image, badges, rating, colour swatches, vendor or subtitle, quick add (single-variant products) and quick view (multi-variant). Options in Theme settings › Cards.

Cart

Cart drawer or cart page (Theme settings › Cart). Add blocks to either: progress bar (free shipping, free gift, spend tiers, custom milestones), promotion message, offer reminders, upsells (Shopify recommendations or hand-picked), discount code, gift wrap, gift message, terms checkbox and app blocks. Checkout is Shopify's own.

Promotions and badges

  • Offers 1–6 (Theme settings) power offer badges, product offer panels, the Multi-buy block, the Promotion block, collection Offer tiles, promotional banners and cart reminders — shown only when the matching Shopify discount is confirmed. See the Promotions Guide.
  • Badges — automatic (sale, sold out, new, low stock, best seller, limited, exclusive, online only, collections, metafield, tags) plus four Badge rules of your own (e.g. Trending, Staff pick), two custom badge styles, size and icons.
  • Campaign mode — a scheduled store-wide campaign with bar, colours and badge.

Design system

Set once in Theme settings and followed by every section. See the Design System Guide.

Responsive design and visibility

  • Every section and block can be shown or hidden separately on desktop (990px+), tablet (750–989px) and mobile (under 750px).
  • Layout sections and Group blocks have columns, direction, alignment, gap and padding per device.
  • Sections and Group blocks have conditions: dates, logged-in or guest customers, countries, cart value, product tags, collections, product stock/sale state, Campaign mode.

Before / after

Available as a section and as a block: before and after images (with phone images), labels, starting position, aspect ratio (with a phone ratio), width, corner radius and border. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard.

Footer

Footer layouts (standard, minimal, editorial, campaign) with brand, menu, text, newsletter and app blocks, social links, payment icons, country/language selectors and copyright.

Apps

Sections accept app blocks where apps are useful (product information, reviews, cart, collection grid, apps section). App embeds are turned on in Theme settings › App embeds.
