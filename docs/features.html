Theme Feature List

Tillhouse by CHRISPSDesign — a modular, premium Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme.

Visual building

  • Sections and blocks you add, remove, reorder, duplicate and hide in Shopify's Theme Editor
  • Design section: a blank canvas of nestable blocks with per-device columns, direction, alignment, gap and padding
  • Sections and blocks grouped by purpose: Layout, Commerce, Media, Content, Marketing, Social proof, Storytelling, Navigation, Product page
  • Show / hide every section and block on desktop, tablet and mobile
  • Scheduling and conditions: dates, customer status, country, cart value, product tags, collections, product stock and sale state, Campaign mode
  • Dynamic sources and metafields across product blocks
  • App blocks and app embeds

Design system

  • 8 demo theme styles: Fashion, Beauty, Streetwear, Electronics, Home & Living, Luxury, Food & Beverage, General Retail
  • 10 style directions that recompose key sections: Luxury, Fashion, Beauty, Streetwear, Electronics & tech, Home & living, Food & beverage, General retail, Editorial, Minimal
  • Colour schemes with sale, success and warning colours; contrast checks in the editor
  • Typography for display, H1–H4, body, small, caption, button and navigation: font, size, weight, line height, letter spacing, capitalisation
  • Buttons, cards, forms, spacing, borders, radius, images, icons and animation settings
  • Reduced-motion support throughout

Header and navigation

  • Header layouts: Standard, Inline, Editorial, Minimal, Campaign
  • Visual mega menus: feature images, products, promotion panel or collection tiles
  • Sticky header (always or on scroll up), transparent header over the home page
  • Mobile drill-down menu drawer with account, country/language and social links
  • Announcement bar with rotating messages and offer announcements

Search

  • Search opens as a panel, side drawer or full screen
  • Popular searches, quick-links menu and featured products before typing
  • Predictive search: suggestions, products, collections, pages and articles
  • Search results page with filters and sorting

Home page and content

  • Slideshow, Hero (editorial, split, full-width, video, promotion), Promotional banner, Promotion tiles
  • Product grid (grid, carousel, editorial), Featured product, Collection list (grid, mosaic)
  • Image with text, Video, Lookbook with hotspots, Testimonials and press logos, Blog posts, Newsletter, Scrolling text
  • Before / after comparison (section and block)
  • Storytelling presets: Product story, Video with text, Full-width media, Features, Materials & care, Specifications, Testimonial, Before / after story

Product pages

  • Two-column layout with gallery left or right, column widths 40/60 to 65/35 or custom
  • Scrolling: auto, sticky information, sticky gallery or normal; tablet stacked or two columns
  • Gallery: thumbnails (left, right, below), slider, grid, stacked, masonry; zoom, lightbox, video, 3D
  • 30+ draggable product blocks including breadcrumbs, subtitle, rating with breakdown, price with savings, stock, variant pickers (buttons, pills, dropdown, swatches; separate size and colour selectors), quantity, buy buttons, Buy it now, payment messaging, delivery estimate with postcode check, pickup, offers, multi-buy, size guide, accordions, tabs, specifications, Complete the look, recommended products, sibling products, share and sticky add to cart
  • Optional product sidebar with shop links, filters, offers and adverts
  • Four product templates: default, editorial, minimal, sale

Collections

  • Collection banner (text, split, overlay) with quick links
  • Filters (Shopify Search & Discovery) in a sidebar or drawer, sorting, density switcher, pagination
  • Editorial grid tiles: image, text, product, video and offer tiles with per-device widths and heights
  • Four collection templates: default, editorial, large image, sale

Product cards

  • Hover image, badges, rating, colour swatches, vendor or subtitle
  • Quick add and quick view

Cart

  • Cart drawer or cart page
  • Progress bars (free shipping, free gift, spend tiers, milestones), promotion messages, offer reminders
  • Upsells, discount codes, gift wrap, gift message, terms checkbox, app blocks

Promotions and badges

  • Six offers: BOGO, buy X get Y, buy more save %, multi-buy price, spend thresholds, free shipping, free gift, sale, custom
  • Offers only appear after you confirm the Shopify discount exists — never misleading customers
  • Promotion block, offer tiles, promotional banners, cart reminders, offer badges
  • Campaign mode: scheduled store-wide campaigns with bar, colours and badge
  • Automatic badges plus four custom badge rules, custom styles, size and icons

Conversion and trust

  • Recently viewed products (no app required)
  • Delivery estimates, trust icons, payment icons
  • Reviews via any reviews app that uses Shopify's standard rating metafields

Quality

  • Accessible markup, keyboard navigation, focus states, ARIA for menus, drawers, dialogs, tabs and sliders
  • Responsive images, lazy loading, no third-party libraries
  • English and French translations

V5 additions

  • Motion: intensity setting (Off, Subtle, Standard, Expressive).
  • Mobile: bottom navigation bar.
  • Search: recent searches on the shopper's device.
  • Collections: Load more and Infinite scroll.
  • Product page: pre-order state from real inventory settings.
  • Sections: FAQ with optional structured data, and Comparison table. New Design-section presets: Stats and Timeline.
  • Theme styles: Conversion, High-impact, Product-focused and Mobile-first.

V6 additions

  • Bundle builder: mix and match with tier pricing, builder page, product block, collection button, promo section and cart grouping (optional; uses Shopify discounts).
  • Trade portal: wholesale landing page and application form, trade order page for Shopify B2B buyers, order-minimum guidance (optional; requires Shopify B2B).
  • Store header layout with inline search, category menu, shortcuts with HOT tags and an accordion menu on phones.
  • New sections: Brand statement, Brand bento, Shop lanes, Product families, Product rail, Editorial collage, Community quotes, Newsletter band.
  • Hand-picked products in Featured collection, product spotlight options, more promo-tile layouts, footer legal notice, solid quick-add bar, payment-icon options.
