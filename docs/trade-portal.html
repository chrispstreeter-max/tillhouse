Trade Portal Guide

The trade portal is a wholesale layer on the same storefront:

  • a public wholesale landing page with an application form;
  • a wholesale order page for approved trade buyers: one fast list with their prices, quantity rules, volume breaks, reorder and an order minimum;
  • an order-minimum check in the cart.

It shares the design system, products and cart with retail. Retail bundles never apply to trade buyers.

Retail Trade
Who everyone who is not a B2B buyer signed-in Shopify B2B customers, while the program is enabled
Price retail price (+ bundles and your retail discounts) the B2B catalog price (Shopify)
Quantities any B2B quantity rules (minimum, case increment, maximum)
Volume bundle tiers B2B volume pricing (quantity price breaks)
Minimum none the program's order minimum (theme guidance, see § 5)

1. Requirements

Shopify B2B is required. The theme never computes or stores a trade price. The price, quantity rules and volume breaks a buyer sees and pays all come from Shopify B2B.

  • Basic / Grow / Advanced: B2B is available, with up to 3 catalogs assigned through B2B markets. Pricing is therefore per market, not per customer.
  • Plus: catalogs can be assigned directly to companies and locations, so pricing can be per customer.
  • Quantity rules and volume pricing are available on all plans.

Check Shopify's current B2B plan details before you promise per-customer pricing.

Access rule (checked on Shopify's server): the customer is a B2B buyer (customer.b2b?) and the wholesale_program entry is enabled. For everyone else, no trade price, catalogue or order data is rendered at all. Nothing is hidden with CSS or JavaScript.

2. Admin setup

  1. Turn on B2B: Settings › Plan / B2B. Follow Shopify's B2B checklist.
  2. Create a catalog: Products › Catalogs. Add the products, set prices, quantity rules and volume pricing.
  3. Assign the catalog to a company/location (Plus) or to a B2B market.
  4. Approve a buyer: Customers › Companies › Add company and location, then add the applicant's email as a customer of that location. When they sign in, they are a B2B buyer.
  5. Remove access: remove the customer from the location. On the next page load they are a retail customer again.

  • Catalogue: the products listed on the order page, still limited by the buyer's B2B catalog.
  • Families: the family filter on the order page, and the product interests on the application form.
  • Excluded products: never listed on the order page.
  • Order page: where "Open wholesale ordering" links.
  • Ordering rules: notes shown in the order summary.

Pages

  • Wholesale: template wholesale, handle wholesale. This is the public landing page with the application form, which uses Shopify's contact form, so applications arrive at the store email.
  • Wholesale order: template wholesale-order, handle wholesale-order.
  • Add a link to /pages/wholesale in your menu or footer. The header's Store layout can also show it as a shortcut.

The application never grants access by itself; approval is always a manual step in admin.

4. Discount safety

By default, Shopify discounts with eligibility All customers, Specific customers or Customer segments apply to both B2B and retail buyers in a blended store.

Before approving the first trade buyer:

  • Set your retail discounts, including bundle tier discounts and storewide codes, to Eligibility › Markets › your retail markets only.
  • Then test a B2B checkout, stopping before payment: catalog prices only, no retail discount.

The theme hides bundles from B2B buyers, but it cannot stop Shopify applying a discount. Only the discount's eligibility setting does that.

5. What is enforced where

Rule Enforced by Shopify at checkout Theme
Trade price yes (catalog) shows it
Product availability yes (catalog publishing) lists the catalogue minus exclusions
Minimum / case / maximum per variant yes (quantity rules) steppers offer only valid quantities; Shopify's refusal is shown
Volume pricing yes price breaks shown; the selection estimate uses them
Order minimum no progress in the summary and cart; checkout button disabled below it

The order minimum is guidance, not enforcement. A disabled button can be bypassed. For a hard block, use either:

  • a Cart and Checkout Validation Function (an app); or
  • the B2B location setting "submit orders as drafts for review".

6. Analytics

Events are published as chrisps_wholesale_<name>, through Shopify Customer events and dataLayer, plus a DOM event. They carry no personal data:

  • page_viewed
  • application_started / application_submitted
  • catalogue_viewed
  • product_viewed
  • order-page interactions

Events about the order page fire only for B2B buyers, because that markup is rendered only after the access check.
