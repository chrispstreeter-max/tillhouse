Product Page Guide

Product templates

The theme includes four product templates: Default product, editorial, minimal and sale. Assign one per product in Products › (product) › Theme template. In the Theme Editor, choose Products in the page selector and pick the template to edit it. Create more with Create template — for example a template for one product type.

The layout

The Product information section has two columns.

Setting Options
Gallery position Left (default) or right — the information column takes the other side.
Column widths 40/60, 45/55, 50/50, 55/45 (default), 60/40, 65/35 or custom.
Content width Narrow, page, wide, full, edge to edge, or a custom maximum.
Column gap 0–120 px.
Tablet (750–989px) Stacked or two columns.
Scrolling (desktop) Auto — the shorter column stays in view; sticky information; sticky gallery; or normal scrolling. Plus a sticky offset below the header and top or centre alignment.
Information width Maximum width of the information column.

Phones always stack: breadcrumbs, gallery, then your blocks in the order you set.

Product information blocks

Every part of the information column is a block. In the left sidebar you can drag blocks to reorder them, add blocks with Add block (group Product page), duplicate or remove them from the block's menu, hide them with the eye icon, and show them per device.

Empty blocks disappear: a rating without reviews, an offer that isn't live, a size guide without content or an empty FAQ render nothing — also in the editor.

Gallery

Layouts: large image with thumbnails (thumbnails left, right or below), single large image (slider), two-column grid, stacked and masonry. Also thumbnail size and gap, image width, aspect ratio (original, square, portrait, landscape or custom), cover or contain, image gap, image counter, arrows, zoom on hover and full-screen lightbox. On phones the gallery swipes edge to edge, inside the page margins, or with thumbnails below. Videos, YouTube/Vimeo and 3D models are supported. The gallery jumps to a variant's image when the variant changes.

Variants

Variant options come from Shopify. Colour swatches use Shopify's category metafield swatches (set on the option values). Sold-out combinations are marked; changing a variant updates price, stock, images and the URL without reloading. Separate Size selector and Colour selector blocks can sit in different places and still select one variant.

Offers

Offers from Theme settings › Offer 1–6 appear only while live and only on products they apply to. The Multi-buy offer block shows tiers such as Buy 2 → 10% off, Buy 3 → 15% off; tapping a tier sets the quantity. The discount itself is applied by Shopify at checkout.

Size guide and FAQ

  • Size guide: create a page with your size chart, then choose it in the block, or create a Page metafield custom.size_guide to give products different guides.
  • FAQ: create a rich-text metafield custom.faq and connect it in a Collapsible content or Tab block. Products without FAQ content show nothing.

Complete the Look

Add the Complete the look block (information column) or section (below the product). Choose products, or create a Product list metafield custom.complete_the_look and connect it with the dynamic source icon so each product has its own set.

Related products

  • Recommended products block (in the column): Shopify's related or complementary products.
  • Related products section (below): Shopify recommendations, same collection, same product type, same vendor, best sellers, new arrivals, manual selection or recently viewed.

Reviews

Install a reviews app that writes Shopify's standard reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count metafields (most do). The Rating block and card stars read them; add the app's block to the Product reviews section for the review list and form. The theme never shows ratings without data.

Storytelling sections

Add Design section presets from Add section › Storytelling above or below the product: Product story, Video with text, Full-width image or video, Features, Materials & care, Specifications, Testimonial and Before / after story. On product templates, block settings can be connected to product metafields so one template tells a different story for each product.

Mobile product pages

Phones show breadcrumbs, the swipe gallery, then your blocks in order. Hide blocks you don't need on phones (eye icon per device), add a Sticky add to cart bar, and preview with the mobile icon in the editor.
