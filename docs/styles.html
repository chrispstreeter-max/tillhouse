Styles Guide

Tillhouse comes in three styles. Each one sets the fonts, colors, buttons, product cards, spacing, header and footer, and home page layout. Everything stays editable in the Theme Editor.

Style Look Suits
Tillhouse Clean and monochrome, uppercase headings, square buttons, portrait product cards Clothing, accessories, design-led brands
Gilt Ivory, black and gold, refined serif headings, wide letter spacing Jewelry, watches, premium goods
Larder Cream, tomato and saffron, rounded and bold Food, drink, gifts and pantry goods

Choose a style

  • When you add the theme: pick the style on the Theme Store before you choose Try theme or Buy theme.
  • Later: in the Theme Editor open Theme settings › Theme style and choose a different style. This changes your design settings, so try it on an unpublished copy first (Online Store › Themes › … › Duplicate).

Fine-tune the look

Theme settings › Style direction recomposes key sections (slideshow, collection lists, product rows and promo tiles) for a type of store: fashion, beauty, streetwear, electronics, home and living, luxury, food and beverage, or general retail. The three styles set this for you, but you can change it at any time.

Empty image slots show the style's built-in photography until you choose your own image. Turn this off in Theme settings › Placeholder artwork.

Before you launch

The styles come with sample text and images.

  • Replace the sample headings, text and images with your own, and remove any sections you don't need.
  • Remove the sample testimonials. They're labeled as samples ("Sample reviews shown for demonstration — replace with your own.").
  • Check delivery and returns messages match what you actually offer.
