Quick Start Guide

Get Tillhouse running on your store in about fifteen minutes. Everything here happens in your Shopify admin — no code.

1. Add the theme

  1. On the Shopify Theme Store, open Tillhouse and choose a style: Tillhouse, Gilt or Larder.
  2. Choose Try theme to add it to your theme library for free, or Buy theme to buy it.
  3. Tillhouse appears in Online Store › Themes, unpublished. Your current live theme is not affected.

2. Open the Theme Editor

Choose Customize on Tillhouse. The Theme Editor has three areas:

Area What it does
Page selector (top) Switch between Home page, Products, Collections, Cart, Search and other templates.
Left sidebar The sections and blocks on the page. Drag to reorder, click to edit, use the eye icon to hide. Theme settings (brush icon) holds the store-wide design system.
Preview (centre) Your store as customers will see it. Use the device icons to preview desktop and mobile.

3. Five-minute basic configuration

  1. Theme settings › Theme style — pick the closest style: Tillhouse, Gilt or Larder (see the Styles Guide). It sets typography, colours, buttons, cards, header and footer layouts and the style direction in one step.
  2. Theme settings › Brand — upload your logo (and a light version for dark or transparent headers), set logo width and favicon.
  3. Theme settings › Colours and Typography — adjust colour schemes and fonts.
  4. Header (left sidebar, Header group) — choose your menu in the Menu block and Menu button block. Build your navigation first in Online Store › Navigation.
  5. Home page — replace the sample text and images in each section, remove sections you don't need, add new ones with Add section.
  6. Footer — set menus, contact text and newsletter.

4. Save, preview and publish

  • Save (top right) stores your changes. Nothing reaches customers until the theme is published.
  • Preview on a real phone: in Online Store › Themes, choose … › Preview on Tillhouse and open the link on your phone.
  • Publish when you have worked through the Setup Checklist. Download a backup first (… › Download theme file).

Good to know

  • Sample content: templates include neutral sample text and placeholder artwork so layouts can be judged. Replace it with your own content. Placeholder artwork shows wherever an image is empty; turn it off in Theme settings › Placeholder artwork.
  • Nothing is invented: the theme never shows fake reviews, ratings, stock levels or discounts. Ratings come from your reviews app; offers only appear once you confirm the matching Shopify discount exists.
  • Keep a backup before large changes: Online Store › Themes › … › Download theme file.
