Frequently asked questions

Do I need any apps?

No. The bundle builder uses Shopify's own automatic discounts and the trade portal uses Shopify B2B. Review apps are optional: Tillhouse shows their star ratings and app blocks when you install one.

How do I install Tillhouse?

On the Shopify Theme Store, choose a style (Tillhouse, Gilt or Larder), then Try theme or Buy theme. It appears unpublished in Online Store › Themes. See the Quick start.

Can I change the style later?

Yes: Theme settings › Theme style. It changes your design settings, so try it on a duplicate of the theme first. See Styles.

What does the trade portal need?

Shopify B2B. On Basic, Grow and Advanced plans, trade prices are set per B2B market; per-customer pricing needs Shopify Plus. See the Trade portal guide.

Does bundle pricing work in every country?

In markets that sell at your store prices. Where a market changes the price (a price adjustment, price list or another currency) the builder tells shoppers bundle pricing isn't available in their region. See the Bundle builder guide.

Will my settings survive a theme update?

Theme updates from the Theme Store keep your settings and content. Code you've edited yourself isn't carried over, so keep a note of any changes.

Can I edit the theme code?

You can, but code changes aren't covered by support and can make updates harder. Always duplicate the theme before editing code, and consider hiring a Shopify Partner for larger changes.
